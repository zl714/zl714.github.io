Required functionality

What the dashboard does

Search

Rescue profiles

Filters dogs for water rescue, mountain or wilderness rescue, and disaster or individual tracking based on the defined requirements.

Explore

Interactive results

Presents shelter records in a filterable, sortable, paginated table with clear status information.

Understand

Chart and map

Summarizes matching animals by breed and maps the location of the selected animal when valid coordinates are available.
